# Ruang Rindu

### A small room for the words that need somewhere to go.

Ruang Rindu is a public letter space for things that are difficult to say out loud: love, longing, gratitude, apology, and goodbye.

Write at your own pace, choose whether to sign your name, and leave a letter when it feels ready. You can also read letters left by other people—slowly, without needing to know who they are.

## What you can do

- **Write a letter** with a title and theme, or keep it simple.
- **Preview your letter** before sending it.
- **Choose how to appear**: use your name, a chosen name, or remain anonymous.
- **Save a draft on your device** and return to it later.
- **Browse the public archive** by searching, filtering by theme, or sorting by date.
- **Read and share individual letters** with a stable link.
- **Report a letter** privately if something needs the moderation team’s attention.
- **Switch between Indonesian and English**, and choose a light or dark appearance.

The interface is available in **Bahasa Indonesia and English**. Letters themselves remain in the language they were written in.

## Before you send

Successfully submitted letters are published automatically in the public archive. They may be read, copied, or shared by anyone who visits Ruang Rindu.

Anonymous posting hides the name you enter from the public letter, but it does not guarantee absolute anonymity. Please do not include passwords, addresses, phone numbers, private correspondence, or other information that could identify you or someone else.

Drafts are saved locally on the device where you write them. Clearing browser data or changing devices can remove an unsent draft. A submitted letter is no longer a private draft: it becomes part of the public archive.

## A note on care

Ruang Rindu is made for honest words and quiet reading. Please do not use it to share personal data, threats, harassment, or anything that could put another person at risk. If a published letter needs attention, use its report action so it can be reviewed privately.
